Frequently Asked Questions

How does Travelo personalize recommendations?
We use a hybrid AI model that analyzes your stated preferences (budget, interests, past trips) and anonymized behavioral signals. The more you interact, the better the suggestions become.
Is my travel data shared with third parties?
No. We never sell personal data. Limited data may be shared only with your explicit consent (e.g., to book a flight via partner API). See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I delete my interaction history?
Yes. Go to Account Settings → Privacy → Delete History. You can also request full account deletion by emailing support@travelo.com.
How do I report a harmful or biased recommendation?
Click the “Report” button next to any recommendation or contact ethics@travelo.com. Our team reviews all reports within 5 business days.
