Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026. Your trust is our compass.

At Travelo (“we,” “us,” or “our”), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our personalized travel recommendation platform, including AI-driven suggestions, itineraries, and destination insights.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver tailor-made travel recommendations, we may collect the following categories of data:

  • Identity Data: name, username, date of birth (for age-appropriate suggestions).
  • Contact Data: email address, travel alerts preferences.
  • Travel Preferences: past destinations, budget range, adventure vs. leisure, dietary needs, accessibility requirements.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, browser, cookies, and interaction analytics to improve our AI models.
  • Usage Data: pages visited, recommendations clicked, feedback provided (e.g., thumbs up/down on suggestions).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Generate personalized destination and activity recommendations using our AI engine.
  • Improve algorithm fairness, accuracy, and relevance based on aggregate feedback.
  • Send you travel inspiration, personalized offers, or service updates (with opt-out available).
  • Ensure platform security, detect fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing Your Information

  • Service partners (e.g., mapping APIs, flight search engines) to fulfill recommendations you choose to book.
  • Legal authorities if required by law or to protect our rights.
  • Aggregated/anonymized data for research or partnership analytics (no identifiable information).

4. Data Retention & Security

We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion. We implement encryption, access controls, and regular audits to safeguard your information.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. You can also opt out of marketing communications. To exercise rights, contact privacy@travelo.com.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies for authentication and performance, plus optional analytics cookies to enhance recommendations. You can manage preferences via your browser settings.

7. Children’s Privacy

Travelo is not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children without parental consent.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of Travelo signifies acceptance of any changes. Major updates will be notified via email or platform notification.

Questions? Contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@travelo.com
