AI Ethics at Travelo

Building responsible, fair, and transparent recommendation systems.

Human-centric AI · June 2025 Charter

Travelo’s AI engine is designed with ethics at its core. We believe that personalized travel recommendations should empower users without compromising privacy, fairness, or sustainability. This page outlines our ethical principles and safeguards.

1. Fairness & Anti-Bias

Our algorithms are regularly audited to avoid discrimination based on race, gender, age, disability, or socioeconomic background. We train models on diverse global travel data and actively mitigate stereotyping in destination suggestions.

2. Transparency & Explainability

You have the right to know why a recommendation was made. Travelo provides simple “Why this?” explanations next to each suggestion — showing factors like your past likes, budget, or trending destinations. We never use black-box models without human oversight.

3. Privacy by Design

Your data is never sold. We use differential privacy techniques and on-device processing where possible. You can delete your interaction history at any time, and our AI models are trained using anonymized aggregates.

4. Sustainability & Carbon Awareness

Travelo’s recommendation engine prioritizes low-carbon options when relevant (e.g., train vs. short-haul flights). We also display estimated carbon footprints for itineraries and suggest eco-certified accommodations.

5. Human Oversight & Feedback Loops

Every AI suggestion can be flagged or corrected. Our team reviews flagged content weekly to improve model behavior. Critical decisions (e.g., accessibility or safety alerts) involve human validation.

6. Inclusive Representation

We actively source travel data from underrepresented regions and local experts. Our content library avoids colonial or overly commercial narratives, highlighting authentic cultural experiences.

7. Ongoing Accountability

We publish an annual AI Ethics Report detailing bias audits, user feedback metrics, and environmental impact. An independent advisory board oversees our compliance.

Have ethical concerns or suggestions? Contact our AI Ethics team: ethics@travelo.com
